How to view your data in GUI tools:

In MySQL Workbench 8.0: 1. Open MySQL Workbench (installed at C:\Program Files\MySQL\MySQL Workbench 8.0).
2. Click your local MySQL connection.
3. Expand the neobank schema in the left navigator.
4. Right click any table (e.g. applications) → Select Rows - Limit 1000.
In MongoDB Compass: 1. Open MongoDB Compass.
2. Paste connection URI: mongodb://127.0.0.1:27017/neobank and click Connect.
3. Click on the neobank database to browse collections as visual documents, view indexes, and filter with queries.
